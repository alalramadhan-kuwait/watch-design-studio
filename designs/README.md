# Reference designs

Studies built in the studio and kept as files rather than as presets, so they
can be opened, changed and saved again without becoming part of the app.

Open one with **More → Import design**.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `valmont-cushion-roman.json` | A cushion Roman study built from a photograph: champagne chapter ring, clous de Paris centre, radial black Romans with IIII at four, blued Breguet hands, printed name on a reserved cartouche. |
| `norel-concept-d-powder-blue.json` | NOREL B6098G-A1 Concept D: 41.0 flush fluted bezel, President bracelet, powder blue matte dial, printed Arabic-Indic numerals, a 38° Arabic day window that leaves room for ١١ and ١, the SW240-1 date under a 2.5× cyclops, blued baton hands. |
| `timekeeper-nivada.json` | Time Keeper × Nivada (Nivada Grenchen F77, Time Keeper edition): brushed steel dial, grain running 12 to 6, in the steel of the case (#DDDFE1; a grained silver-white dial before 4.2.0), applied rose-gold Arabic-Indic numerals at all twelve hours, rose-gold pencil hands and long-tailed seconds, "Nivada" over GRENCHEN in rose gold and black, TIME KEEPER above 6, on its case photograph with the black rubber strap. Nivada's sheet 103277_3, Version A: laid out for the Ø28.00 opening (refitted from 27.50 in 4.7.0), hands at the sheet's lengths (hour and minute W36017, 8.5 and 12.0 mm; seconds 13.0 mm). Its maker's sheet details (4.8.0): design number 103277_4, hand reference W36017, the dial marked (changed) and the script (same as before), as on the approved sheet. Opened from **Your watches** on the Case step. |
