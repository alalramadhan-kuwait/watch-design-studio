# Reverso Study

Figures lifted from Jaeger-LeCoultre's own product photograph of the Reverso
Classic Monoface, reference Q2548140 (8000 px square, straight on, about
210 px a figure; the photograph is not kept here). No sub-dial, so every
figure is whole. The 2 comes from the 12 (the minute hand touches the 2 at
2 o'clock), the 0 and 1 from the 10 and 1, the others from their own places,
with `lift.py` and `spec.json`. Every figure measured square; weight as
printed; round edges dip 10 units; outlines fitted with smooth curves between
their corners.
