# Lange 1815 Study

Figures lifted from A. Lange & Söhne's own product photograph of the 1815,
reference 206.032 (straight on, about 310 px a figure; the photograph is not
kept here). No straightening was needed. The 1 and 2 come from the 12, the 0
from the 10, the others from their own hours, with `lift.py` and `spec.json`.
The small seconds cuts the 6 in half on every 1815, so the 6 is drawn as the 9
turned upside down, as this face pairs them (`derive` in `face.json`). Every
figure measured square; weight as printed; round edges dip 10 units;
outlines fitted with smooth curves between their corners.
