# Max Bill Study

Figures lifted from a wrist photograph of a vintage Junghans Max Bill
(Hodinkee, "Why I still love my Junghans Max Bill"; the photograph is not
kept here). The dial, seen at an angle, was straightened through the eleven
printed minute numbers round its edge (`dial.fit_affine`, `affine.txt`), turned
a further 2.2° when the 1, 4, 5 and 6 all measured that lean, and each figure
lifted upright (`lift.py`, `spec2.json`): the hour hand covers part of the 10's
0, which is rebuilt from its left half (the 0 is symmetric), and the dust on
the crystal is filtered out (`speck_px`). Built with `build.py` and
`face.json`: one height, weight as printed, the 8 turned −3° and the 9 +2.5°,
round edges dipping 10 units, outlines fitted with smooth curves between their
corners (`spline`).
