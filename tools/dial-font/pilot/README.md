# Pilot Study

The IWC Big Pilot's Type A figures, applied lume, light on a black dial
(`"dark": false`). Every figure is traced whole from a figure no hand covers:

- 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9: IWC's own product photograph of the Big Pilot's Watch
  43, IW329301 (straight on, about 150 px a figure);
- 0, 2, 7: in that photograph the hands lie over them (10:10), so they come
  from a photograph in Hodinkee's "A Week On The Wrist" review (070A2428),
  where the hands stand at 10:15 and all three are clear. That photograph
  draws strokes heavier than IWC's by 0.03 of the figure's height, measured on
  the 5 in both, so those three are thinned by 11.5 units a side to the set's
  weight (`grow_per` in `face.json`).

No figure is rebuilt, mirrored or repaired. The photographs are not kept here.
Every figure measured square; the 3's flat top sits on the line; outlines
fitted with smooth curves between their corners.
